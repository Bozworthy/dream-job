## Exclusions
* Fidelity

## Posting qualification criteria
1. **Employer-side verification (required)** — the role is currently live on the employer's own careers site or official ATS (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, etc.), and the application resolves to the employer's legitimate application flow. A posting that fails this is excluded outright.
2. **Recruiting-investment verification (ranking signal)** — the same role is also actively distributed through another substantive recruiting channel (LinkedIn, Indeed, or another credible paid/recruiting platform). A careers-page listing alone doesn't prove the company is actively investing in filling the role, so a posting that passes criterion 1 but lacks this second signal is not excluded — it gets down-ranked relative to postings that have both.

## Operating constraints
* Nothing goes into resume/portfolio copy without direct verification against the SOT — strict metric fidelity, no fabrication
* Fit/risk concerns get flagged once, then Claude executes on Jon's call — no relitigating after he's decided
* Every posting gets checked against criterion 1 (required) and criterion 2 (ranking signal) before anything is built
