## Getting work now while still chasing the target roles

Your own numbers point the way. About 34 cold applications produced one target-level human response (Capital One). The two processes that moved fast were IQ Fiber, a local role with a human reply in under 24 hours, and Onward Search, where an agency came to you.

1. **Run a contract track alongside everything else.**
   - Agencies place content designers at large tech and finance companies on contract, and contract hiring takes weeks, not months.
   - Register with 3 or 4 creative and UX staffing firms: Onward Search (already active), Aquent/Vitamin T, Creative Circle, and 24 Seven. Apex Systems and TEKsystems post conversational design contracts.
   - Send each one the same short note: your title, conversational AI and fintech, remote, available now, and a rate floor.
   - A remote contract also fills the "Present" line on your resume with current work in the field.
2. **Use referrals for the target roles.**
   - Cold applications have produced one response in 34. Referrals usually get a human to read the resume.
   - For every target role, find a former Credit Karma or Intuit colleague, or a second-degree LinkedIn connection, at that company before applying. Ask for a referral or a 15-minute call.
3. **Check Jacksonville employers directly.**
   - Local roles moved fastest for you (IQ Fiber).
   - Large Jacksonville employers with design and digital teams: Florida Blue/GuideWell, Availity, Fanatics, VyStar, Mayo Clinic, and Baptist Health. (FIS is held out until the Fidelity question is settled.)
   - Their job sites weren't reachable through the standard Workday endpoints this pass, so I'll map them for the next search.
   - Your exclusion list says "Fidelity." Does that cover FIS (Fidelity National Information Services), or only Fidelity Investments?
4. **Apply in the first days a posting is up.**
   - Your one callback came from applying 7 days after posting. Most of today's strong fits are 2 to 5 weeks old.
   - Running the full search on Tuesday as the SOP says, and applying within 72 hours of a posting going up, should help.
5. **Fix the portfolio before more people look.**
   - Capital One-side visitors have been reading your case studies all week.
   - The /ckm-redesign summary box still shows the fabricated "+30% adoption / -25% support calls" figures.
