## How to read this
- **Applied**: date of the ATS confirmation email. When Jon shares a submission, log the date he gives instead.
- **Ref code**: the `?ref=` tag on the portfolio link in that resume. Blank means no tagged link was found in analytics.
- **Portfolio signal**: outside visits only. Jon uses macOS, so Windows, iOS, Android and Linux visits are other people. macOS visits are ambiguous (could be Jon or a reviewer).
- **Days**: days from applied to the latest employer response.
- **Confirmed** means an ATS email exists. A Drive resume alone means the resume was built, not that it was submitted.
