## How to read this
- **Applied**: Jon applies right after each resume is generated and confirms in chat. A tailored resume in Drive counts as an application. The date is the ATS confirmation email when one exists, otherwise the Drive resume date.
- **ATS email**: whether a confirmation email exists in Gmail. "None" means Jon applied but the employer's system never sent a confirmation.
- **Ref code**: the `?ref=` tag on the portfolio link in that resume.
- **Portfolio signal**: outside visits only. Jon uses macOS, so Windows, iOS, Android and Linux visits are other people. macOS visits are ambiguous. No portfolio data exists before Aug 6.
- **Days**: days from applied to the latest employer response, or days waiting as of 9/30.
